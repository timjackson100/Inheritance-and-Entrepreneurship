\begin{abstract}
Seven self-contained potential Data Study Group challenges, drawn from the 18 October 2026 Turing submission and subsequent UK inheritance work. Each section specifies a five-day research question, a data package to prepare by \textbf{31 December 2026}, measurable sprint outputs, preparation tasks and a \emph{separate longer-term extension}. Sections 1--3 are the original proposed challenge strands; Sections 4--7 are additional candidates, not extra commitments. The programme is data-focused and excludes theoretical modelling. A dataset's existence does not guarantee permission for Turing participants to use it.
\end{abstract}
