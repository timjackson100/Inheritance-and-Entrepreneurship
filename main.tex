\begin{abstract}
This is a \emph{data-only} working overview based primarily on the Turing Data Study Group proposal to be done in March 2027. It separates three proposed five-day DSG tasks: extraction from wills, inheritance timing in ELSA, and economic outcomes around inheritance-tax reforms. Additional, clearly distinct sections set out future UK data access, an extension of the IFS inheritance-and-inequality studies, outcomes for recipients, and lifetime gifts and estate distribution. All data sources and access arrangements are proposals unless stated otherwise. 
\end{abstract}
