\section{Sprint candidate 1: Extracting bequests from wills}
\label{sec:wills}

\subsection{Five-day question}
How accurately can open-weight AI extract people, relationships, assets and intended bequests from wills, with evidence for every assertion? Score entity/relation F1, supporting-text accuracy and unsupported claims. A gift specified in a will is \emph{not} a confirmed receipt.

\subsection{Data package by December 2026}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \textbf{Firm core:} IE4Wills, containing 45 authentic US wills and 16,018 human annotations, with published splits, reference labels and baseline extraction code (subject to CC BY-NC 4.0 terms).
\item \textbf{UK adaptation test, if cleared:} 10--20 England-and-Wales probate wills with independently reviewed labels and documented legal processing/hosting permissions. Report UK results separately; the small sample cannot establish national accuracy.
\item A schema for testators, beneficiaries, relationships, specific/residuary/conditional gifts and source passages; a reproducible scoring script and a short test plan.
\end{itemize}

The sprint remains a valid US benchmark and domain-transfer feasibility exercise if the UK pilot is not ready. No identifiable modern wills will be sent to unauthorised external model services.

\subsection{Five-day deliverables}
Compare an IE4Wills baseline with open-weight prompting/schema adaptation and, if approved, UK examples. Return accuracy, uncertainty, error categories, computational cost and annotated outputs permitted by the licences.

\subsection{To do by 31 December 2026}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Confirm IE4Wills licence, sample splits and benchmark reproduction.
\item Define the extraction schema, evaluation protocol and independent UK labels.
\item Seek an initial HMCTS probate sample; confirm permission and safe hosting before including it.
\item Prepare scripts, documentation and a small dry run of the full scoring pipeline.
\item Decide by year-end whether the UK adaptation test is cleared or remains an extension.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Longer-term extension}
Build a larger and more representative UK wills corpus through HMCTS, solicitors, will-writing services (for example Farewill/Dignity and Co-op Legal Services), and estate-administration firms (for example Kings Court Trust). Registries such as the National Will Register may help locate records, but do not necessarily hold full text. Approach providers with a proposal for a governed pilot and ask about signed/revoked wills, codicils, dates, coverage, permissions and selection bias.

Most importantly, seek \emph{final estate accounts} and beneficiary payment dates: these distinguish intended bequests from realised net receipts and could support subsequent inheritance-distribution research. Such person-level work requires a separate lawful, custodian-approved project.
