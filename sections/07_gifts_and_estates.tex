\section{Sprint candidate 7: Lifetime gifts and loans}
\label{sec:gifts}

\subsection{Five-day question}
Who gives and receives substantial lifetime gifts and family loans, at what ages, and for which purposes? How do these transfers compare with reported inheritances in timing and distribution across wealth and parental socioeconomic groups?

\subsection{Data package by December 2026}
Use the \textbf{WAS (UKDS 7215; 2006--2022)} gifts and loans modules, including reported giving/receipt, amounts, age, relationship, stated purpose, wealth, income, parental background (where present), wave and weights. Published IFS work by Boileau and Sturrock provides established definitions and benchmarks. The survey records significant transfers (including gifts or loans of at least \pounds500 in relevant waves), not every small or informal flow; verify thresholds and questions for each wave.

The UKDS EUL source already exists. Prepare a clean extract, variable dictionary and survey-weighted pilot before the sprint. Giving and receiving reports from different households are not automatically matched sides of one transaction.

\subsection{Five-day deliverables}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Weighted transfer rates, real values and concentration by age, wealth, income and parental background.
\item Breakdown by giver--recipient relationship and stated purpose, particularly housing.
\item Distinguish gifts from loans and compare the age profile of lifetime transfers with inheritances.
\item Reproduce or update selected IFS descriptive charts using the available WAS waves, reporting recall and survey limitations.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{To do by 31 December 2026}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Confirm the precise gifts-given, gifts-received, loans and inheritance questions across WAS rounds.
\item Obtain the EUL extract, harmonise amounts, inflation adjustments, thresholds and survey weights.
\item Check sample sizes by transfer type, purpose, relationship and age group.
\item Select published IFS benchmarks and produce an initial summary table.
\item Package the data/code under permitted Turing access; flag questionnaire changes requested but not yet collected.
\item Make sure data sprint participants have access to the approved datasets, documentation and required computing environment before the sprint.
\item Prepare documents for data sprint participants explaining the tasks, suggested teams, division of work, coordination points and possible expansions or stretch goals.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Longer-term extension}
Link donor-side gifts, later estates and realised beneficiary allocations where a lawful source exists. Study unequal transfers to siblings, care-related late-life wealth depletion, business succession, tax planning, charitable legacies and the substitution between gifts and bequests. These require estate accounts, richer family relationships or restricted administrative records and should not be promised as five-day outputs.
