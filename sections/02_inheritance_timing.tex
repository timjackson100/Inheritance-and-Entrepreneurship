\section{Strand 2: Age at inheritance and parental death}
\label{sec:timing}

\subsection{Research question}
At what age do individuals receive parental inheritances, and how closely does their age at the second parental death approximate actual reported inheritance timing?

This is a \emph{within-survey} exercise. It does not require, or permit, identification of anonymised respondents against probate records.

\subsection{Primary dataset: ELSA}
The English Longitudinal Study of Ageing (UK Data Service Study 5050) is the proposed primary source. Wave 6 retrospective inheritance histories and parental-death questions are promising starting points, but the accessible End User Licence files, exact variables and overlap must be audited before the study can promise estimates.

The required fields are:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item respondent birth year (or an authorised age/year equivalent) and survey-provided longitudinal identifier;
\item receipt year, source and, if available, amount of inheritance;
\item mother's and father's survival status and death years;
\item birth cohort, socioeconomic characteristics, sampling weights and interview/wave timing;
\item optional repeated measures of wealth, housing, employment and retirement outcomes.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Constructed timing measures}
For a respondent with a reported parental inheritance:
\[
a_{\mathrm{receipt}}=t_{\mathrm{receipt}}-t_{\mathrm{birth}}.
\]
Where both parental death years are observed:
\[
a_{\mathrm{second\ death}}
 =\max(t_{\mathrm{mother}},t_{\mathrm{father}})-t_{\mathrm{birth}}.
\]
The key proxy diagnostic is
\[
\Delta a=a_{\mathrm{receipt}}-a_{\mathrm{second\ death}}.
\]
This difference is computed only when both measures are available and properly defined. Death of the second parent is \emph{not} assumed equivalent to an inheritance: transfers may occur at first death, before death, later than probate, or never.

\subsection{Analytical tasks}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item Audit the ELSA release, questionnaire, variable names, access tier, survey weights and relevant wave overlap.
\item Establish sample sizes for inheritance recipients, respondents with both parental death years, and respondents with both the timing measures.
\item Estimate distributions of age at reported inheritance and at second parental death, with uncertainty intervals.
\item Quantify proxy differences and missingness, and examine variation by birth cohort and socioeconomic group.
\item Account for parents still alive (right-censoring), recall error, parental deaths before panel entry and selective receipt of inheritances.
\item If the sample permits, describe subsequent wealth, housing or work trajectories; do not interpret bereavement comparisons as causal inheritance effects without further design.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Additional datasets (not DSG core)}
\begin{description}[style=nextline]
\item[Wealth and Assets Survey (WAS; UKDS 7215)]
Independent analysis of reported inheritance receipts and values alongside wealth, property, debt and pensions. Its exact variables and the viable longitudinal sample need a separate audit.
\item[Understanding Society (UKDS 6614)]
Potential replication with household transfer and economic data, conditional on confirming suitable parental-death and inheritance-timing variables.
\item[ONS life tables]
Optional public benchmark for whether observed parental mortality patterns are plausible; not for person-level linkage.
\end{description}

\subsection{Expected outputs}
A reproducible variable and access inventory; a sample-flow and missingness table; age distributions by cohort; a direct assessment of agreement between reported inheritance timing and second-parent death; and permitted analysis code plus disclosure-safe results.

\subsection{Preparation status and dependencies}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \textbf{Primary dependency:} appropriate UK Data Service access and a Turing-compliant analysis environment.
\item \textbf{Scientific dependency:} verification that receipt-year and both parental death-year measures overlap in a useful sample.
\item \textbf{Fallback:} a documented variable/access audit, analysis protocol and synthetic code example rather than unsupported empirical estimates.
\item \textbf{Boundary:} no attempt to re-identify respondents or link survey records to named wills, Gazette notices or probate records.
\end{itemize}
