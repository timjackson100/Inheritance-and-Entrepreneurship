\begin{abstract}
This is a \emph{data-only} working overview based primarily on the Turing Data Study Group proposal designated for submission on 18 October 2026. It separates three proposed five-day DSG tasks: extraction from wills, inheritance timing in ELSA, and economic outcomes around inheritance-tax reforms. A final, clearly separate section lists longer-term UK data possibilities, including WAS and HMRC. Proposed data sources and outputs are not assumed to have been obtained or completed. The theoretical model is deliberately excluded.
\end{abstract}
